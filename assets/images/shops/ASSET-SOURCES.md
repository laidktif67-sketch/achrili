# Shop logo assets

These files are bundled locally and are referenced by the shop cards in `index.html`.

## Brand-mark sources verified

- **AliExpress** — SVG mark sourced from the Simple Icons project: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons
- **eBay** — SVG mark sourced from the Simple Icons project: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons
- **Etsy** — SVG mark sourced from the Simple Icons project: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons

These are vector brand marks, not necessarily the brands' full-colour official lockups.
