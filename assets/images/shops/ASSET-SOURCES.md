# Shop logo assets

All eight shop-card images are stored locally in this repository and referenced by `index.html`; no remote image URLs are needed at runtime.

## Source/provenance status

- **AliExpress**, **eBay**, and **Etsy**: vector marks sourced from the Simple Icons project (https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons). These are community-maintained brand marks and may be monochrome rather than full-colour official lockups.
- **Temu**, **Amazon**, **SHEIN**, **Walmart**, and **ASOS**: local SVG wordmark illustrations in the project. They were refined for consistent sizing and accessibility, but have **not** been verified as original artwork from the respective brands. Do not describe these five files as official assets.

## Official references for future replacement/verification

- Temu press assets: https://www.temu.com/pa/press.html
- Amazon press logo library: https://press.aboutamazon.com/logos
- Walmart Brand Center: https://brandcenter.walmart.com/brand/brand-identity/wordmark
- SHEIN corporate brand page: https://www.sheingroup.com/brands
- ASOS plc media library: https://www.asosplc.com/news-and-media/media-library/

If official source files are obtained, preserve the downloaded artwork and attribution/provenance where applicable. Keep final assets bundled locally; do not add runtime dependencies on external image URLs.
